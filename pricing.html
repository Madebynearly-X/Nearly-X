Plan details & terms

How payment plans work.

When does my project start and launch?

Your project is scheduled as soon as you accept the written scope and complete the first payment. Launch occurs once testing is complete, typically within 7–14 business days.

What happens if a monthly payment is late?

Each payment includes a 5-day grace period. If a payment reaches 7 days overdue, the website is temporarily paused until the outstanding payment and a R250 reconnection fee are settled.

When do I own the website and domain?

You own 100% of your website code, design assets and domain within 5 business days of your final payment. You can also settle early at any time with no penalty.

What are monthly content updates?

Each monthly update covers text edits, image swaps, news posts, or price adjustments to keep your site fresh while your plan is active.
