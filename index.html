Good to know

Before we begin.

How soon can we start?

Projects are typically scheduled within 1–2 weeks of confirming the written scope and receiving the initial deposit or first payment.

What do I need to prepare?

We'll guide you through the essentials. Typically you will provide your logo, service descriptions, pricing, contact details, and any images you have. We can also source clean, professional photography.

Can I spread payments over time?

Yes. We offer transparent 3- and 6-month payment plans bundled with managed hosting and monthly updates. Explore all payment plans →

Do you work with businesses outside Pretoria?

Yes. While we are based in Pretoria, we work with clients across South Africa (Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban) and internationally via Google Meet, email, and WhatsApp.
