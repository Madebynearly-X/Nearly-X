Good to know

Before we begin.

What will you need from me?

You'll provide your business and service information, any brand assets and content you want to use, and feedback during the agreed review rounds. We'll confirm the exact inputs in the written scope.

Who pays for the domain and hosting?

Domain and hosting fees are separate unless your written quote says otherwise. Accounts should be registered in your name so you keep control of them.

What does Website care include?

Plans start from R500/month and include WordPress, theme and plugin updates, a monthly backup and basic security check, plus up to two hours of small content changes. Hosting, domain renewals and new features are separate.
