LINKEDIN / TEXT-ONLY MEMBER POSTS

Connect LinkedIn.

Connect an owner-authorized LinkedIn member account to publish reviewed text posts. Company pages and media posts are not included.

Only approved LinkedIn drafts can be published. Public posts cannot be undone from this dashboard; use the emergency pause to stop new publish requests. Disconnecting removes the stored token here; revoke app access separately in LinkedIn if needed.

CANVA / VIDEO TEMPLATE WORKFLOW

Create from your Canva template.

Connect your Canva account to fill an existing video-capable brand template and export an MP4.

The template and video asset must already exist in your Canva account. Use exact field names from its autofill dataset. Canva creates a new filled design; review it in Canva before exporting.

ADOBE EXPRESS / VIDEO EDITOR

The interactive editor needs Adobe business approval, an HTTPS app domain, and an approved Embed SDK client ID.

Adobe's editor is interactive: you select or upload the source media and save/export from Express.
