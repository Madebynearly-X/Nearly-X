The 3-Step Process

How your project moves forward.

Every website follows a disciplined, communicative roadmap designed to keep delivery fast and stress-free.

01 / BRIEF & DISCOVERY

Get clear

We learn about your business, audience, and goals. We confirm the page architecture, deliverables, fixed price, and schedule in writing before any deposit is paid.

02 / DESIGN & BUILD

Make it real

We shape the structure and aesthetic, craft responsive mobile and desktop experiences with micro-interactions, and share an interactive staging link for your direct feedback.

03 / TESTING & LAUNCH

Test and hand over

We audit core user paths, page speed, form delivery, and cross-device responsiveness. Once approved, we connect your domain, deploy live, and provide a comprehensive handover.

Typical Project Timelines
